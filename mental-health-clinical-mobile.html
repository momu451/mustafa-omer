Safety / Treatment Review

Risk screenAsk directly about suicidal ideation, self-harm thoughts, impulsivity, protective factors and whether distress is escalating.
Medication and therapy planReview medication response, sleep, side effects, adherence and whether NHS Talking Therapies / trauma-focused therapy / further review is appropriate.
Fit note / ESA contextThe issue is not diagnosis alone; it is whether symptoms impair reliable work capacity and whether a fit note extension is clinically justified.
